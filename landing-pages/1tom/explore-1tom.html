01 / Fit, both directions

Who this is for, and who it isn’t

Worth exploring if you:

  • Have run a crew, a branch, a territory, or a P&L
  • Are comfortable being in the business every day, not overseeing it from a distance
  • Want a service business with demand that does not wait for a good economy
  • Are willing to spend real time evaluating this before deciding anything

Not a fit if you:

  • Want passive income or an absentee investment
  • Need a guaranteed return or a promised income figure
  • Are not in a position to evaluate a $238,000 to $454,000 investment
  • Are looking for a job with 1-Tom-Plumber rather than ownership of a location

03 / The unflattering part

What ownership actually asks of you

This is an owner-operator business, and the title means what it says. In the early years you are in it: hiring technicians and keeping them, quoting jobs, chasing receivables, checking the work, taking the call yourself when a tech is out and a customer’s basement is filling. You are not reviewing a report from somewhere else. You are the operator.

Emergency service is the business, and emergencies keep emergency hours. Burst pipes and failed water heaters do not respect evenings, weekends, or the second week of your vacation. As the operation matures you build a team and an on-call rotation — but early on, some of those calls reach you. If that sounds unworkable, believe yourself now rather than eighteen months in.
