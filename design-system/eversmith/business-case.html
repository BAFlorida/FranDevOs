Complication

Lead cost is falling and unit cost is not.

Cheaper leads have not produced cheaper units, which means the savings are landing in the wrong part of the funnel.

The dashboard shows lead cost down. It does not show that the leads getting cheaper are the ones that were never going to close.

Volume is not the constraint. Qualification is. Until a candidate is scored against lender criteria and operator fit before the discovery call, additional spend buys additional noise, and the platform pays for it seven times over.
