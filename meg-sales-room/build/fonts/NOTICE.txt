Embedded typefaces
==================

The MEG pages embed three typefaces as subset woff2 data URIs so that no
outbound font request is needed. All three are licensed under the SIL Open
Font License, Version 1.1, which permits embedding in documents.

  Anton         - Vernon Adams, Cyreal.  https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Anton
  Montserrat    - Julieta Ulanovsky et al. https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Montserrat
  Nunito Sans   - Vernon Adams, Jacques Le Bailly.
                  https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Nunito+Sans

Full licence text: https://openfontlicense.org/

Per tokens.css these stand in for the brand faces: Anton for Abolition,
Montserrat for Gotham, Nunito Sans for Avenir Next. If 1-Tom-Plumber licenses
the real faces, replace the woff2 files here, rerun build/subset_fonts.py and
rebuild. Note that Abolition, Gotham and Avenir Next are commercial faces whose
licences must be checked before they are embedded as data URIs.
